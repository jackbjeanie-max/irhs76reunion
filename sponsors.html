Help Us Make the 50th Reunion Extra Special

We are now welcoming sponsors, donors, and door prize contributors to help make the Indian River High School Class of 1976 50th Reunion a memorable celebration.

Your support can help with:
Decorations, door prizes, keepsakes, special reunion touches, and other event expenses that help make the evening memorable for our classmates and guests.

Ways to Support the Reunion

Business Sponsor

Businesses and organizations are invited to support the reunion and help us create a special evening for the Class of 1976.

Classmate or Friend Donation

Any contribution, large or small, is appreciated and helps us add thoughtful touches to the celebration.

Door Prize Contribution

Gift cards, merchandise, services, or other appropriate door prize donations are welcome.

Recognition

We would be happy to recognize sponsors and donors on the reunion website and, when timing allows, in reunion-related materials. Recognition details may vary depending on the type and timing of the contribution.
