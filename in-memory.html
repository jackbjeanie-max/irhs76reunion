In Memory

Forever a Part of Our Brave Family

We are preparing a respectful tribute to classmates who are no longer with us.

To help us keep the tribute accurate and respectful, you may submit information about a deceased member of the Indian River High School Class of 1976.

Please Include

  • Full name
  • Confirmation that they were a member of the IRHS Class of 1976
  • A link to an obituary, funeral-home notice, or other public notice
  • An optional short remembrance or paragraph
  • An optional photo, if you have permission to share it
Photo Permission:
If you submit a personal or family photograph, please confirm that you have permission to share it for inclusion on the reunion website.

We will use obituary or funeral-home links to help verify names and details. We may summarize public information rather than copying obituary text directly.
