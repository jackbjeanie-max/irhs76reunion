Share Your Memories

Submissions Are Now Being Accepted

We're creating a special place for the photos, stories, and memories that made our Indian River years unforgettable.

If you have old school photos, yearbook pictures, earlier reunion photos, newspaper clippings, or a favorite story from your Indian River years, we'd love to hear from you.

What to Send

  • Your name and graduation year
  • The photo or photos you would like to share
  • The approximate year the photo was taken, if known
  • Names of people pictured, if known
  • A short paragraph, story, or memory to go with the photo
Permission to Share:
By submitting photos, stories, or other material, you confirm that you own the material or have permission to share it, and you give the IRHS Class of 1976 50th Reunion Committee permission to display it on the reunion website and in reunion-related materials.
